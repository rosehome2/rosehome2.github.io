🧩 mad-framework - Build Real Business Apps Without Writing Code

A free, open-source toolkit that gives you more than 250 ready-made screen elements, live-updating pages, user accounts, document export and an AI helper — all working together out of the box.

📖 What Is mad-framework?

mad-framework is the engine that powers MadBuilder applications. In plain terms: it is a large collection of pre-built building blocks — buttons, forms, tables, calendars, charts, menus, pop-up windows and much more — that you can assemble into a working business application instead of building everything from scratch.

Think of it the way you would think of a very large box of Lego bricks. Each brick already does its job perfectly. Your task is only to decide which bricks go where. That means creating an app for booking appointments, tracking inventory, managing customers or collecting form entries becomes a matter of arranging pieces rather than programming them.

Because the project is open source, it is completely free to download, use and change. Nothing is hidden behind a subscription, and you can look inside every part of it to understand how it works.

✨ What You Can Do With It

  • 250+ ready-made screen elements. Every piece of a typical business screen already exists — you pick what you need and place it on your page.
  • Pages that react instantly. When someone types in a field or clicks a button, the page updates immediately without a full reload. This makes your app feel fast and modern.
  • User accounts and permissions. Sign-in, sign-up, password resets, roles and access rules are handled for you, so you can decide who sees what.
  • Documents and PDF output. Turn on-screen information into a clean, printable document such as an invoice, a report or a certificate.
  • An AI copilot. A built-in assistant that helps you assemble screens, suggests layouts and explains how pieces fit together.
  • An MCP server. A standard connection point that lets AI tools and other software talk to your application safely.

💻 Before You Begin

You do not need any programming knowledge, but your computer should be reasonably up to date. Please check these basics before you start:

  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer with a 64-bit processor.
  • At least 4 GB of memory, though 8 GB or more gives you a smoother experience.
  • About 1 GB of free disk space for the application and its supporting files.
  • A stable internet connection for the initial setup and for the AI copilot features.
  • A modern web browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox, updated to its latest version.

🧭 Your First App in Five Simple Steps

Once the application is on your computer and running, the rest is mostly clicking and choosing. Here is the natural order most people follow.

  1. Start the application and open your workspace. When everything is ready, your browser will show a welcome screen. This is your workspace — think of it as the desk where you will build things.
  2. Create your first page. Choose a blank page and give it a name that makes sense to you, such as "Customer List" or "Booking Form". Nothing is permanent, so you can rename it later.
  3. Add components from the library. Open the component list and drag whatever you need onto the page. A text field, a date picker, a table, a submit button — each one lands exactly where you drop it and can be moved again at any time.
  4. Adjust the look and the behaviour. Every component has a small settings panel. Here you change the label text, choose whether a field is required, set the colours, and decide what should happen after someone presses a button.
  5. Turn on accounts and access rules. When your pages are ready, switch on user accounts and give each group of people the level of access they need. Some will be able to edit, others will only be able to view.

🎨 Inside the Component Library

The heart of mad-framework is its collection of over 250 pre-built pieces. They are grouped so you can find what you need quickly:

  • Inputs and forms — text boxes, drop-downs, date and time pickers, file uploads, check boxes and sliders.
  • Data displays — sortable tables, cards, lists, progress bars and summary tiles.
  • Navigation — side menus, top bars, breadcrumb trails, tabs and pagination.
  • Feedback — pop-up notifications, confirmation dialogs, loading spinners and empty-state messages.
  • Media and documents — image galleries, file previews and printable document layouts.

Because the pieces are designed to work together, you never have to worry about whether two elements will fit. They share the same spacing, colours and behaviour, which is why finished apps look consistent without extra effort.

🔐 Accounts, Permissions and Safety

Any application that holds real business information needs proper access control. mad-framework includes identity and access management from the start, which means the following are already built in and do not need to be created by hand:

  • Sign-up and sign-in screens with password handling.
  • Password reset by email.
  • Roles such as administrator, editor and viewer.
  • Page-level and field-level rules that decide who may see or change what.

For anyone who has ever built an internal tool, this is usually the most tedious part. Here it is already prepared, so you simply assign people to the right role and let the system enforce the rest.

🤖 AI Copilot and the MCP Server

The AI copilot is your in-app assistant. You describe what you want in ordinary sentences — "add a customer feedback form with a rating and a comment box" — and it proposes the right components, placed in a sensible order. You stay in control: accept what you like, adjust what you do not, and undo anything with a single click.

The MCP server is a connection standard that lets approved AI tools and other software read from and write to your application through a defined, controlled channel. In practice it means you can later connect assistants, automations or reporting tools without hand-building a custom bridge for each one.

📄 PDF and Document Output

Many business tasks end with a piece of paper or a file that needs to be sent somewhere. mad-framework can produce PDF documents directly from your pages and tables. Set up a layout once — a header with your company name, a table of items, a total row, a signature area — and every future document comes out looking identical. Invoices, quotations, delivery notes, certificates and monthly reports all follow the same simple pattern.
